# Give to Caesar, Give to the Poor

Jesus once said:

> “Give to Caesar what is Caesar’s, and to God what is God’s.”

This teaching is often understood as a reminder to be responsible in society. If something is owed to the government, such as taxes, then it should be given. But Jesus’ words also point us to a deeper truth: everything ultimately belongs to God.

## If We Give to Caesar

Caesar was a ruler with wealth, power, and authority. In today’s world, “Caesar” can represent governments, systems, or people who already have much.

If we are willing to give what is required to those in power, then we should be even more willing to give to those in need.

## We Should Give to the Poor

The poor often have little or nothing. They may need food, clothing, shelter, kindness, or help in difficult times.

Giving to the poor is not only an act of charity. It is an act of love, mercy, and faith.

When we help the poor, we are showing that we care about what God cares about.

## Giving to the Poor Is Giving to God

Jesus taught that when we care for the hungry, the thirsty, the stranger, the sick, and the needy, we are serving Him.

So when we give to the poor, we are also giving to God.
