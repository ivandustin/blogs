# Like a Wireless Prayer

A laptop can send a message without any visible wire.  
You cannot hear the signal, but the server receives it.

Prayer is like that in a greater way.

We may speak softly, or even only in our heart, but God hears.

> “Before a word is on my tongue, behold, O LORD, you know it altogether.”  
> — **Psalm 139:4**

God is high above all things, yet He is also near to those who call on Him.

> “The LORD is near to all who call on him, to all who call on him in truth.”  
> — **Psalm 145:18**

Even when we pray in secret, God hears.

> “Pray to your Father who is in secret. And your Father who sees in secret will reward you.”  
> — **Matthew 6:6**

But prayer is not just sending words into the sky.  
Jesus is the One who brings us to God.

> “I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me.”  
> — **John 14:6**

Because Jesus died for our sins and rose again, we can come to God with confidence.

> “Let us then with confidence draw near to the throne of grace.”  
> — **Hebrews 4:16**

So even the tiniest prayer is not lost.  
God hears.  
God knows.  
And through Jesus, we can come near to the Father.

> “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.”  
> — **Romans 10:13**
